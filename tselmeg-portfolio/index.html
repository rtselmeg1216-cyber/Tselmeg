About me

The short version, in my own words.

I'm an ENFJ through and through — I have a big circle of friends, and I'm happiest when I'm organizing something for the people around me or cheering them on. I transferred to Mongol Aspiration International School in 2023 after coming from School №93, and it's been one of the best decisions of my school life.

I'm the eldest of four girls — I have three younger sisters — and I live with my grandfather during the school year. When summer break comes around, I head back to my family's hometown to spend real, unhurried time with everyone I don't get to see as often.

Outside of class, you'll usually find me on a volleyball or basketball court, out for a run, or mid-workout with my headphones in. I'm drawn to economics because I like understanding why people, markets, and countries make the choices they do — and I want to keep asking that question at university.
